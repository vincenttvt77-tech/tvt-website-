Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TVT Capital LLC ("TVT Capital," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you interact with our website at tvtcapital.com, submit a funding inquiry, communicate with our team, or otherwise engage with our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity information: name, business name, role / title
  • Contact information: email address, telephone number, mailing address
  • Financial inquiry data: capital amount requested, use of proceeds
  • Diligence data (after term sheet acceptance): financial statements, tax returns, bank statements, KYC information, beneficial ownership information
  • Communications: emails, call recordings (where lawful and disclosed), and message exchanges
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device type, and aggregate site analytics

2. How we use information

  • Evaluate, underwrite, structure, and fund your financing request
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry, term sheets, and active facilities
  • Perform required identity verification, anti-money-laundering, and KYC checks
  • Operate and improve our website and services
  • Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests

3. SMS & mobile communications

If you provide a mobile telephone number and check the SMS consent box on any TVT form, you consent to receive calls and text messages from TVT Capital and its representatives at that number, including via automated dialing technology, prerecorded or artificial-voice messages, and artificial-intelligence (AI) voice agents, regarding your funding inquiry, application, term sheet, or active facility.

Consent is not a condition of any financing or purchase. Message frequency varies based on account activity. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You may reply HELP at any time for help, or STOP to opt out of further messages. Opting out does not affect your existing financing.

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile opt-in data or SMS consent information to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Information sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only: with service providers bound by confidentiality obligations; with co-lenders, lender coordination parties, or successors in interest in connection with your financing; as required by law; or with your written consent.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to perform our services and comply with legal, tax, and regulatory record-keeping obligations.

6. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we hold. Financial documents are transmitted and stored using industry-standard encryption.

7. Your choices

You may decline to provide information, withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP, request a summary of the information we hold about you, or request deletion of information not subject to retention obligations.

8. Children

TVT Capital provides commercial financing only. Our services are not directed to individuals under 18.

9. Changes

Material changes will be posted to this page with a revised "last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@tvtcapital.com.
