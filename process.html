Process

Simple process. Real people.

Banks can take weeks. We usually come back the same business day, and often with a term sheet within 24 hours. No runaround, and no guessing where your deal stands.

Start the Intake →
I

Send us the deal ~2 minutes

Two-minute structured intake — capital amount, use of proceeds, top-line financials. No documents required at this stage.

II

We review it Typically same day

A senior underwriter typically calls the same business day. Indicative pricing, structure, and gating questions — the conversation that filters dead deals out before paperwork starts.

III

Get your options Typically within 24 hours

Term sheet in plain English — structure, pricing, covenants, and conditions to close, all on one document.

IV

We check the details

Financials, KYC, and structure docs. We work with your CFO, controller, or QofE provider directly. No third-party portal.

V

Get it done

Sign the credit agreement. Subject to standard conditions, funds wire to the operating account. We handle UCC filings, payoffs, and paperwork with other lenders.

Timing reflects typical experience and is not a commitment. See Disclosures.
