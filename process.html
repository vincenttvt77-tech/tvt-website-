Process

From intake to wire, at operator speed.

Banks can take weeks to decide. TVT typically returns an indication the same business day and an indicative term sheet inside 24 hours — with every step subject to underwriting, diligence, and standard conditions to close.

Start the intake →
01

Intake ~2 minutes

Two-minute structured intake — capital amount, use of proceeds, top-line financials. No documents required at this stage.

02

Indication Typically same day

A senior underwriter typically calls the same business day. Indicative pricing, structure, and gating questions — the conversation that filters dead deals out before paperwork starts.

03

Term sheet Typically within 24 hours

Indicative term sheet in plain English — structure, pricing, covenants, and conditions to close, all on one document.

04

Diligence

Financials, KYC, and structure docs. We work with your CFO, controller, or QofE provider directly. No third-party portal.

05

Close & fund

Sign the credit agreement. Subject to standard conditions, funds wire to the operating account. We handle UCC filings, payoffs, and intercreditor paperwork.
