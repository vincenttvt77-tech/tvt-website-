The Firm

How we work

TVT Capital exists to do one thing: deploy capital at the speed real businesses actually move.

We are a direct lender with a senior underwriting team that reviews every file before it leaves the building. We answer our phone. We do not back-door brokers.

Direct means our underwriter is the decision — not a syndicate, not a fund-of-funds, and not an investment committee that sits once a month.

What “direct lender” actually means

A real underwriter reviews your file, calls you with an indication, and issues a term sheet — typically inside 24 hours. If we can fund the deal, we do. If we can't, we say so quickly.

Who we lend to

Our deepest expertise is with established operating companies — too complex for community banks, too small for the bulge bracket. That is where TVT does its most considered work. But we also fund strong operators outside that band: if you produce real cash flow and have a clear use of proceeds, we want to hear about it.

What you can expect from us

A term sheet often within 24 hours. Diligence that respects your time. Documentation that doesn't take six rounds. The bar is high because the operators we serve don't have time for anything less.
