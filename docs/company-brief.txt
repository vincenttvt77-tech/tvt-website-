One other important change
I would not use a lot of phrases like these throughout the website:

"Deep middle market expertise"
"Indicative term sheets"
"Subject to standard close conditions"
"Specialty finance"
"Competitive economics"
"Intercreditor work"
"Diligence calls"
"Capital solutions"
"Facilitated transactions"
"Comprehensive financing solutions"
They're not necessarily wrong, but they make the website sound older and more institutional than it needs to be.

Instead, TVT's voice should sound like:

We understand business. We move quickly. We tell you what we can do. And we stay involved until the deal gets done.

That is much easier for a business owner, broker, ISO, or referral partner to understand—and it still makes TVT sound sophisticated and credible.
