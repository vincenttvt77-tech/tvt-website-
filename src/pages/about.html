The Firm

How we work

TVT Capital exists to do one thing: get businesses the capital they need, at the speed they actually move.

We’re a direct lender. Our own team reviews every file before it goes anywhere. We answer the phone, and we don’t go around our partners.

Direct means our underwriter is the decision — not a syndicate, not a fund-of-funds, and not an investment committee that sits once a month.

What “direct lender” actually means

A real underwriter reviews your file, calls you, and tells you what we can do — often within 24 hours. If we can fund it, we do. If we can’t, you’ll know quickly.

Who we lend to

We work best with established businesses that are too complex for a community bank and too small for a big investment bank. But we fund plenty of businesses outside that range too. If you have real cash flow and a clear plan for the money, we want to hear about it.

What you can expect from us

A term sheet often within 24 hours. Questions that respect your time. Paperwork that doesn’t take six rounds. Business owners don’t have time for anything less.
