Built by operators, for operators.

TVT Capital exists to do one thing: deploy capital at the speed real businesses actually move.

We are a direct lender with a senior underwriting team that reviews every file before it leaves the building. We answer our phone. We do not back-door brokers.

Direct means our underwriter is the decision — not a syndicate, not a fund-of-funds, not a committee that meets next Thursday.

What we mean by direct.

A senior credit officer reviews your file, calls you with an indication, and issues an indicative term sheet — typically inside 24 hours. If we can fund the deal, we do. If we can't, we say so quickly.

What “middle market” means here.

Our deepest expertise is with established operating companies — too complex for community banks, too small for the bulge bracket. That is where TVT does its most considered work. But we also fund strong operators outside that band: if you produce real cash flow and have a clear use of proceeds, we want to hear about it.

The bar we hold ourselves to.

Indicative term sheets typically inside 24 hours. Diligence that respects your time. Documentation that doesn't take six rounds. The bar is high because the operators we serve don't have time for anything less.
