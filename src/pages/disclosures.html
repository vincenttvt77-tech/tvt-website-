Disclosures

Commercial financing only

TVT Capital LLC provides commercial financing exclusively to business borrowers. We do not extend consumer loans, residential mortgages, or any form of credit subject to the federal Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z) consumer-protection provisions.

No commitment until executed agreement

No discussion, indication, term sheet, letter of interest, or other communication from TVT Capital constitutes a binding commitment to lend. A binding obligation arises only upon TVT Capital's execution of a definitive credit agreement, subject to all conditions precedent stated therein.

Marketing and timing claims

References on this website to specific timing — including "indications typically inside 24 hours," "typically funded in days," "same-day," "same-week," or comparable phrases — describe typical experience and are not commitments. Actual response, approval, and funding timelines depend on factors including: the completeness and accuracy of information provided; the borrower's responsiveness to diligence requests; the structure, complexity, and risk profile of the requested facility; legal, regulatory, and compliance requirements; third-party diligence inputs; intercreditor or senior-lender coordination; and the negotiation and execution of definitive documentation. No specific timing outcome is promised or guaranteed.

References to capital deployed, deal counts, renewal rates, and other historical metrics are based on TVT Capital internal records as of the date of publication. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Comparative statements regarding bank or other lender timing reflect general industry experience and are not directed at any specific competitor.

Representative transaction information

Deal types, sectors, states, and structures shown on this website — including those in the ticker displayed at the top of each page — are representative of the kinds of transactions TVT Capital underwrites. They are not a list of specific closed transactions, do not identify any borrower, and make no claim as to when any particular financing was funded.

Indicative pricing and terms

Pricing, structure, and terms communicated during the inquiry, indication, or term sheet stages are indicative and subject to credit underwriting, diligence findings, and documentation.

Eligibility

TVT Capital extends credit to U.S. businesses with verifiable cash flow and a clear use of proceeds. Eligibility is determined at TVT's sole discretion.

Geographic coverage

TVT Capital lends to qualified borrowers located in all 50 U.S. states.

Personal guarantees

Personal guarantees may be required for certain TVT facilities, determined deal by deal and disclosed in the term sheet.

State-specific commercial financing disclosures

Where required by state law, TVT Capital provides a separate commercial financing disclosure document at or before the time of executing a financing agreement.

Fair-treatment statement

TVT Capital evaluates credit applications based on the merits of the business, its operating fundamentals, the use of proceeds, and the credit profile of the principals. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, receipt of public assistance, or exercise of any right under applicable consumer-credit laws.

Brokers and intermediaries

TVT Capital works with licensed brokers, ISOs, and capital advisors through our Partner Program. Partners do not have authority to bind TVT Capital. No partner is authorized to charge upfront fees to a prospective borrower on behalf of TVT Capital.
