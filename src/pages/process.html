Process

II

III

If the deal fits, we lay out the structure, the terms and the next steps in plain English, on one page.

We’ll ask for financials and a few documents, and work directly with whoever handles your books. No third-party portal.

V

Get it done

Once you sign and standard conditions are met, the money goes to your account. We handle the filings, payoffs and paperwork with any other lenders.
