Legal · Licensing

Licensing

TVT Capital LLC operates as a commercial-purpose lender to U.S. businesses. The licensing requirements applicable to a particular financing depend on the state(s) of operation of the borrower and the structure of the credit facility.

Coverage

TVT Capital extends commercial financing to qualified borrowers in all 50 U.S. states. We do not provide consumer-purpose credit.

Commercial financing disclosures

Several U.S. states have enacted commercial financing disclosure statutes (including California, New York, Utah, Virginia, and Georgia). Where required by applicable state law, TVT Capital delivers a state-specific disclosure document at or before the time of execution of a financing agreement. These disclosures include, as applicable: total financing amount, disbursement amount, finance charge, total cost of capital, estimated APR (where required), payment amounts and schedule, and prepayment policy.

Requesting licensing information

To request specific licensing or registration information for a state, contact info@tvtcapital.com with your state of operation and the nature of your inquiry.

Regulatory updates

State commercial financing regulation is evolving. This page is updated as new regimes are enacted or amended. The applicable law for a given financing is the law in effect at the time of execution of the credit agreement.
