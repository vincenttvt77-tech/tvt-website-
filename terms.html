Legal · Messaging

Messaging Terms

These Messaging Terms govern the calls and text messages you may receive from TVT Capital LLC at any telephone number you provide to us. By providing a phone number on a TVT Capital form and consenting to be contacted, you agree to these Terms.

Program description

The TVT Capital messaging program sends transactional and account-related calls and text messages relating to your funding inquiry, application, term sheet, diligence, and any active financing. We do not send promotional broadcasts unrelated to your interaction with us.

Consent

By submitting a form on tvtcapital.com that includes a phone number and checking the affirmative consent box, you agree to receive recurring calls and text messages from TVT Capital and its representatives at the number provided, including calls and messages delivered via automated dialing technology, prerecorded or artificial-voice messages, and artificial-intelligence (AI) voice agents. Consent is not a condition of any purchase, financing, or service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the stage and activity of your inquiry or facility.

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply per your wireless plan. TVT Capital does not charge a fee to send or receive messages.

Opt-out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, END, QUIT, CANCEL, or UNSUBSCRIBE to any text message we send. After you reply STOP, we will send one final confirmation message. Opting out does not affect your existing financing.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, call (555) 555-5555, or email info@tvtcapital.com.

Supported carriers

Messages are deliverable on most major U.S. wireless carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy & mobile data

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your mobile opt-in data or SMS consent information to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at info@tvtcapital.com.
