Section I

About TVT Capital

What is TVT Capital?
TVT Capital is a direct lender to U.S. businesses, providing bridge loans, growth capital, working capital, and second-lien facilities from $50K to $25M. We underwrite every deal in-house and lend nationwide.
Are you a bank?
No. TVT Capital is a non-bank direct lender. Underwriting happens in-house, which is why we can move faster and price more flexibly than a regulated commercial bank.
How much have you deployed?
TVT Capital has deployed over $4B across 6,000+ deals.
Section II

Eligibility & sizing

What size loans does TVT Capital provide?
$50,000 to $25,000,000, structured around the transaction.
Who qualifies as a borrower?
Operating U.S. businesses with verifiable cash flow and a clear use of proceeds.
Section III

Process & timing

How fast can a business actually close?
Indicative pricing typically comes back same-day and indicative term sheets typically inside 24 hours. Closing follows underwriting, diligence, and standard conditions — timelines depend on the deal.
Do you require a personal guarantee?
It depends on structure. Sponsor-backed and well-collateralized senior deals are frequently completed without a full PG.
Section IV

Products

Bridge loan vs. growth capital — what's the difference?
A bridge loan is short-term financing (3–24 months) sized to be repaid by a defined event — an equity raise, an acquisition close, a refinance, or an exit. Growth capital is built for sustained expansion with longer maturities and amortization aligned to free cash flow.
I already have a senior bank lender. Can you still help?
Yes. Our second-lien product is purpose-built to sit behind an existing bank facility without disturbing the senior relationship.
Section V

Pricing & partners

Are there upfront fees to apply?
No. There is no retainer, application fee, or diligence fee on application or indication.
Do you work with brokers and ISOs?
Yes — through our partner program: competitive commissions, direct underwriter access, and a no-backdooring commitment written into every partner agreement.
